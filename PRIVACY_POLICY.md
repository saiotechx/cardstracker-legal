2.
Financial Calculations — To calculate credit health estimates, payoff simulations, and spending analysis

3.
Notifications — To send payment reminders and milestone celebrations (if enabled)

4.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All financial data is stored locally on your device:

•
Card information

•
Spending entries

•
Payment history

•
Budget settings

We do NOT upload your financial data to our servers unless you explicitly enable cloud sync (future feature).

4.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

•
Encrypted local storage using device keychain/keystore

•
HTTPS encryption for any data transmitted

•
Regular security audits

•
No storage of sensitive data (SSN, full card numbers, passwords)

4.3 Data Retention

•
Local Data: Stored on your device indefinitely until you delete it

•
Analytics Data: Retained for 90 days, then automatically deleted

Support Data: Retained for 1 year for troubleshooting purposes
