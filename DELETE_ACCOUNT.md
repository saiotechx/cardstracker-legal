CardStracker — Account & Data Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your CardStracker account and all associated data, please follow the steps below.




How to Request

 Deletion

Send an email to saiotechx@gmail.com with the following details:

•
Subject line: CardStracker Account Deletion Request

•
Your email address associated with your CardStracker account

•
A statement that you wish to permanently delete your account and all data

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 30 days.




What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our servers:

Data Type

Account profile (name, email)

Permanently deleted

Permanently deleted
Monthly snapshots
Permanently deleted
App settings and preferences
Permanently deleted







Partial Data Deletion

If you wish to delete only specific data (for example, a single card or spending entry) without deleting your entire account, you can do so directly within the CardStracker app at any time. No request is needed for in-app data management.




Data Retention

All account data is permanently and irreversibly deleted within 30 days of receiving your deletion request. No backups or copies are retained after this period.




Contact

For any questions about your data or this process, contact us at saiotechx@gmail.com.
